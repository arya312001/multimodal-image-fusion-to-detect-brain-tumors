The Process Of The User Selecting An Option

The process of selecting an appropriate wavelet family and fuse combination for a specific purpose. This involves carefully considering various options and making a decision based on the specific requirements and objectives. The selection process includes evaluating different wavelet families, assessing their properties and characteristics, and determining which one would best suit the desired outcome. Additionally, the process involves considering different fusion techniques and combinations, analyzing their effectiveness in achieving the desired result, and ultimately choosing the most suitable option that optimally combines the wavelet family and fuse combination.
